# Privacy Policy

**Pitch & Dice**  
Last updated: May 13, 2026

## What this app does

Pitch & Dice is a single-player cricket dice game for iOS and Android. You choose teams, shot types, bowling, and field settings, then play through a match with commentary and audio. The app can save an in-progress match, keep a short local match history, and remember whether audio is muted so you can continue where you left off.

## Personal data

Pitch & Dice does not collect personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other identifying information, and it does not send that kind of information to us.

Gameplay progress and preferences are stored only on your device for app functionality. They are not uploaded to our servers.

## Accounts

No account is required. You can use the full game without signing in or creating a profile.

## Advertising and tracking

Pitch & Dice does not use third-party advertising or tracking. The app does not include ad networks, analytics SDKs, or similar services that follow you across apps or websites.
